Data coverage & limitations

This map compiles publicly available reference information. Well and wastewater facility locations are approximate. Water and sewer mains, pipe sizes, hydrants, water tank locations and sewer pump stations were not available for mapping and are not represented.

Electric infrastructure is sourced from OpenStreetMap; service territory is a historical 2018 reference. FCC broadband is aggregated 2025 data, not address-level service confirmation. The growth corridor is a project study area, not an official ETJ.

For planning reference only. Confirm utility availability, location and capacity with the respective providers before design or investment decisions.
